## Doubts for a human

- **Line 69 / lab narrative.** The lab's own hardcoded final print line — "No client write was lost or corrupted: only heartbeats were dropped." (`run.out` 10, `split-brain.go` 254) — carries the same error. Fixing the chapter prose alone leaves the lab output inconsistent with the fix; the lab's printed line should be corrected and `run.out` regenerated (out of scope for a report-only pass).
- **Line 87, "partial synchrony".** The term is used without a citation. It is the standard classification of Raft's model (safety in the asynchronous model, timing only for liveness), and F33/F34 support the substance, but a stricter reader might object that Raft's `broadcastTime ≪ electionTimeout ≪ MTBF` bound is a stronger, more specific synchrony assumption than textbook DLS partial synchrony. Consider citing or a one-clause qualifier.
- **Line 69, "Node C follows B".** A mild modeling gloss: the naive lab never records *which* node a follower follows — any heartbeat simply resets its timer. Acceptable as prose, but if the topic later formalizes "leader" as tracked per-follower state, a short qualifier ("in this naive model, following B just means B is the only node whose heartbeats reach C") would pre-empt confusion.
